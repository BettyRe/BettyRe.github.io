Credits & Acknowledgements

This environment was created as part of my Computer Science and Digital Media studies, for the module Grundlagen virtueller Welten (Fundamentals of Virtual Worlds). I would like to thank the artists and developers whose work helped bring this environment to life.

Original work

Architectural design and custom modeling of the hill-integrated house, modular walls, doors, windows, chimneys and exit sign; environment composition, level design, and integration and adaptation of interactive systems. Third-party models, textures and tutorial code are acknowledged below.
